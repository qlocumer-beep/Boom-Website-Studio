FAQ

Got Questions?

How long does it take to build my website? ▼

Most websites are completed within 10–14 days from the time we receive your content. Complex projects with multiple pages and custom features may take a bit longer, but we'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.

Do I have to pay monthly fees? ▼

No. You pay once, and you own the website forever. The only ongoing costs are your domain name (around $15/year) and hosting (from $5/month). We can recommend affordable options and help you set it up.

What if I need changes after launch? ▼

We offer affordable monthly support plans starting from $99/month, or you can request one-off changes at an hourly rate. We can also teach you how to make basic updates yourself — your choice.

Do you build on Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress? ▼

No. Every website we build is custom-coded from scratch. This means faster load times, better SEO, unique design, and no platform lock-in. You get clean code that you fully own — not a template.

What industries do you work with? ▼

We work with all types of local businesses — restaurants, cafes, tradies, salons, gyms, real estate, professional services, and more. If you need a website that brings in customers, we can help.

Will my website show up on Google? ▼

Yes — SEO is built into every site we build, not added as an afterthought. We handle technical SEO, page structure, load speed, and local optimisation so Google can find and rank your site. Results improve over time, but we set you up properly from day one.
